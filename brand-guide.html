01 —

Principles

Unflinching

No hedged language. Oversized type. High-contrast layouts. Silence where others would fill with noise.

Editorial

Treat the campaign like a magazine cover. Pull-quotes, numbered policy, italic display serif for emotional punches.

Monumental

Type at architectural scale — 128–200px headlines. Content that physically dominates the viewport.

02 —

Color

Sampled from the torch mark. The palette is intentionally narrow — two brand hues, one ink, one warm paper. Everything else is a tonal shift of those four.

05 —

Buttons & Motion

Contribute → Join the Movement → Read the Policy → Watch Rally →

Cursor

Custom blob cursor. Magnets to primary CTAs. Mix-blend-difference over dark hero tiles.

Transitions

Framer Motion tile entrance, staggered 60ms. GSAP ScrollTrigger pins hero overlap.

Hover

Tiles lift 2px. Arrow slides 6px. Images reveal on policy hover. No drop shadows — use 1px hairlines.
